About Vincenzo

We are a small team of book lovers who want everyone to find a story worth reading.

Our Story

From a shelf of favourites to a reading community

Vincenzo began in 2022 as a tiny list of favourite books shared between friends at university. People kept asking for more recommendations, so we built a place to collect, rate and share them.

Today, thousands of readers use Vincenzo to discover new authors, keep track of what they read, and talk about the stories they love.
